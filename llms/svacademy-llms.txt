# SV Academy

> SV Academy is a tech-sales training and talent-placement organization that runs an intensive Sales Development Representative (SDR) program and places pre-trained, job-ready graduates with employers at no placement fee. It has trained 6,000+ graduates across the U.S. and Canada and is a portfolio company of 500 Global.

SV Academy operates two audiences: job seekers who complete the SDR training program (no prior experience or degree required), and employers who hire and onboard pre-trained SDR graduates.
